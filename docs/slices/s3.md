## What it adds

- `ground_truth/census.py`: one bucket at a time, one SQLite transaction per bucket with a `done` mark, so an interrupted run resumes and a finished bucket makes no request (AC-14).
- `ground_truth/x402scan_filter.py`: x402scan's own pass-through collapse, ported (source O6).
- Unmarked facilitator transactions: found from each listed address's nonce, by bisecting only the block ranges where the nonce rose more than the marked transactions explain (`census.gap_blocks`).
- `ground_truth/reconcile.py`: AC-4 per bucket, and the files `run` writes. `ground_truth/waterfall.py`: W0 and W1 steps that add up.
- `ground_truth/rpc.py`: lookups by hash, block or nonce rotate between publicnode and blastapi (both keyless); `eth_getLogs` stays on publicnode.

## What slice 3 found that changes later slices

1. **x402scan already collapses router chains** (its issue #1024, source O6). The "routers double count" story from slice 1 is fixed upstream; the waterfall shows it as a merged-legs step, not as an x402scan error.
2. **x402scan counts facilitator transactions with no x402 marker** (O7): 730 in the window; 43 USDC transfers among them, $158.22. W1 removes them as `unmarked`.
3. **x402scan misses about 0.4% of rows**, almost all coinbase, never the other way. Identifying the exact transactions needs a per-transaction x402scan listing; the paid API is out of scope.
4. **W1 barely moves the total.** Whatever x402 volume is not real will show up in W2–W5 (slice 4), not in attribution.
5. **The free nodes.** publicnode alone throttled sustained 50-call batches (about 13 minutes a bucket); 1rpc.io hit a free usage cap mid-run and was dropped; blastapi plus publicnode ran at 3 to 9 minutes a bucket.
