## Falsifiers (`docs/preregistration.md`)

1. **No wash story.** W2–W5 remove 88.43% of W1 dollars, well above 5%. Not triggered. Most of it is the one pair above, which W4 removes for being a payee with one payer, not for a proven wash pattern.
2. **The published share holds.** Kept is 11.57% of W0 dollars; Visa/Artemis kept 11.05% (since launch, all chains). The gap is 0.52 points, inside the 5-point band, so the pre-registered headline applies: the published adjusted share holds on a later window, labelled as a different window. The match rests on four payments; see above.
3. **Replication.** W0 is 0.09% above x402scan's sum of the same 47 buckets (slice 3). Not triggered.

W5 removes 32.38% of W1's payments but 0.65% of its dollars. That is above the 25–30% of transactions a Coinbase employee attributed to leaderboard farming (source D6), which counted all chains in August, not Base payments in this window.
